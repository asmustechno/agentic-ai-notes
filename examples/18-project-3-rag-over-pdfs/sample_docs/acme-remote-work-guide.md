# Acme Corp remote work guide

This is a fictional sample document written for the Agentic AI Notes RAG project. Acme Corp is not a real company.

## Who can work remotely

Once you have finished your three-month probation period, you can work remotely for up to three days a week. Agree your remote days with your manager and record them in the HR portal.

## Core hours

Everyone is available between 10:00 and 15:00 in their local time zone. Outside core hours, plan your day as you like, as long as your team knows how to reach you.

## Equipment

IT provides a laptop, a headset and a monitor. Each employee also has a home office budget of €500 every three years, for things like a desk or a chair. Claim it through the expense tool with a receipt.

## Working from another country

You can work from another country for up to 20 working days per calendar year. Request it in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance. Some countries are excluded for tax and legal reasons; the HR portal shows the current list.

## Security at home and on the move

Always use the company VPN on public or shared Wi-Fi. Lock your screen whenever you step away. Report a lost or stolen device to the security team within 24 hours, even if you find it again later.
