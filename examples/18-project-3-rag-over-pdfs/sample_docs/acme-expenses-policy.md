# Acme Corp travel and expenses policy

This is a fictional sample document written for the Agentic AI Notes RAG project. Acme Corp is not a real company.

## Who this applies to

This policy applies to every employee and contractor who travels on Acme business. Managers are responsible for checking that claims from their team follow it.

## Booking travel

Book flights and trains through the Acme travel portal, ideally at least 14 days before you leave. Fly economy class on flights shorter than six hours. Premium economy is allowed on flights of six hours or more. Trips that will cost more than €2,000 in total need written approval from a director before you book.

## Hotels

The nightly hotel limit is €150 per night, including taxes, in most cities. In high-cost cities (London, New York, Zurich and Tokyo) the limit is €250 per night. If you need to go over the limit, for example during a large conference, ask your manager before you book and attach their approval to the claim.

## Meals

When a trip includes an overnight stay, you can claim up to €60 per day for meals. Alcohol is never reimbursed. Meals with clients are covered separately: note the client's name and company on the claim.

## Local transport

Use public transport where a reasonable option exists. Taxis and ride-hailing are allowed when there is no reasonable public transport, when you carry heavy equipment, or for any journey after 22:00. Car rental needs manager approval.

## Receipts and deadlines

Attach a receipt for every expense of €25 or more. For smaller expenses, a short note in the expense tool is enough. Submit your claim within 30 days of the end of the trip. Claims submitted later than 90 days after the trip are not paid.
